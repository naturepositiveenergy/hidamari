# Hidamari (working title, ひだまり村)

Cozy village life-sim browser game (PWA) for the general public: the player moves to a small Japanese village and, over one in-game year, builds a community solar "power garden" (solar and nature in one) together with the residents. It should be fun first; the learning comes through the residents' stories, the nature diary and a short village notebook. Languages: Japanese and English.

This is a separate game from Solar Commons (`C:\dev\solar-commons`, the stakeholder workshop game), which stays unchanged. Its research, tools and code may be read and reused (copied), never changed.

- Plan: approved on 10 November 2026. Phases: 0 concept and art style, 1 small playable test ("is it fun?"), 2 core systems, 3 story and content, 4 polish and release.
- Decisions taken: separate project and website; original pixel art drawn in code (16 × 16 tiles, own palette, no downloaded assets, except one free pixel-style Japanese font with an open licence, decided on 10 November 2026); one in-game year of about 2–3 hours, played over several sessions; working title "Hidamari" / 「ひだまり村」.
- Design document: `docs/design.md` (written in Phase 0)

# Playtest guide: the whole game (Milestone 4.2)

This guide replaces the Phase 1 guide (`docs/playtest-phase1.md`), as decided on 12 November 2026. The questions of this test: **"Is it fun? Is it clear? Would you play on?"**, now for the whole year. Please test with 3–5 people from the public, ideally some who don't usually play games, on computers and on phones.

## Before the test

- **The game:** https://naturepositiveenergy.github.io/hidamari/ on a computer or a phone. (On a computer you can also double-click `index.html` in the project folder.)
- **Version:** the title screen's "About" window shows the version number. Write it down for each tester, so their notes match the right version.
- **A new game for each tester:** "New game" replaces the saved game on that device. Use a different device or browser for each tester, or finish one tester before the next starts.
- **Two sessions of 30–45 minutes per tester.** A whole year takes 2–3 hours, so nobody needs to finish it. Ask testers to **go to bed in the game before they stop**: the game saves every evening.
- **Settings to leave alone at first:** let testers find the settings (text size, furigana, sounds) by themselves. Note whether they do.

## During the test

- Give only this introduction: *"This is a test of a new village game. Play as you like, and think aloud if you can. I'll only help if you're stuck for a long time."*
- **Don't help for the first 5 minutes.** Write down where people hesitate or get stuck.
- Notes to take:
  - Device (computer or phone), language, age group, how often they play games
  - Did they find out how to walk, talk and use the action button by themselves?
  - Did they find build mode, and did they understand the four views (wind, wildlife, light, water)?
  - Did they understand why some plots need a town meeting, and what the worries list is for?
  - Which residents did they talk to most? Did they see a heart event (a small sun over someone's head)?
  - Did they join a festival? Did they open the nature diary or the village notebook?
  - Where did they smile, laugh or say "oh!"? Where did they sigh, skip text or look bored?
  - How many days did they play in each session? Did they stop early, and why?
  - Did they change any settings (text size, furigana, sounds)?

## Six questions after the second session

Ask in the tester's language. Write down their words as exactly as you can.

| # | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What did you enjoy most? | いちばん楽しかったことは何ですか？ |
| 2 | Was anything confusing or hard? (the controls, or knowing what to do next) | わかりにくかったこと、難しかったことはありましたか？（操作や、次に何をすればいいかなど） |
| 3 | What do you think the panels, hedges, meadows and ponds in your gardens do? And what did the town meeting change? | 庭のパネル、生け垣、草地、池には、それぞれどんな役割があると思いましたか？説明会では何が変わりましたか？ |
| 4 | Which resident would you like to get to know better, and why? | もっと知りたいと思った住民は誰ですか？それはなぜですか？ |
| 5 | Was the pace right: the length of a day, and the amount of text? | ペースはちょうどよかったですか？（1日の長さや、文章の量など） |
| 6 | Would you like to play until the end of the year? (yes / maybe / no, and why) | 1年の終わりまで遊んでみたいですか？（はい／たぶん／いいえ、その理由も） |

- how clear the gardens, the town meeting and the goals are

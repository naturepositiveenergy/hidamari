# Playtest guide: Phase 1 test (3 days)

The question of this test: **"Is it fun? Is it clear? Would you play on?"** Please test with 3–5 people from the public, ideally some who don't play games.

## Before the test

- **On a computer:** copy the whole `hidamari` folder (for example on a USB stick or as a zip file), then double-click `index.html`. It works without internet.
- **On a phone:** phones can't easily open a folder, so the test has to be on a website. The simplest way is GitHub Pages (needs a public repository, to be decided). Until then, test on computers only.
- Start a **new game** for each tester. ("New game" replaces the saved game on that device.)
- A full test takes about **15–20 minutes** (3 days of about 5 minutes each).

## During the test

- Give only this introduction: *"This is a short test of a new village game. Play as you like, and think aloud if you can. I'll only help if you're stuck for a long time."*
- **Don't help for the first 5 minutes.** Write down where people hesitate or get stuck.
- Notes to take:
  - Device (computer or phone), language, age group, how often they play games
  - Did they find out how to walk, talk and use the action button by themselves?
  - Did they read Mr. Oda's letter? Did they find the field sign and build mode?
  - Did they meet both Gen-san and Midori? Did they clear Gen-san's leaves?
  - Did they get the nature diary and draw at least one animal into it?
  - What did they build on the field, and why?
  - Where did they smile, laugh or say "oh!"? Where did they sigh or look bored?
  - How long did they play? Did they stop before the end?

## Five questions after the test

Ask in the tester's language. Write down their words as exactly as you can.

| # | English | 日本語 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What did you enjoy most? | いちばん楽しかったことは何ですか？ |
| 2 | Was anything confusing or hard? (the controls, or knowing what to do next) | わかりにくかったこと、難しかったことはありましたか？（操作や、次に何をすればいいかなど） |
| 3 | What do you think the panels, hedges and meadows on your field do? | 畑のパネル、生け垣、草地には、それぞれどんな役割があると思いましたか？ |
| 4 | Which resident would you like to get to know better, and why? | もっと知りたいと思った住民は誰ですか？それはなぜですか？ |
| 5 | Would you like to play a whole year? (yes / maybe / no, and why) | 1年分、続けて遊んでみたいですか？（はい／たぶん／いいえ、その理由も） |

## After all tests

Collect the notes in one place (for example a table with one row per tester) and share them with Claude. The answers decide what changes before Phase 2: the controls, the pace of a day, the amount of text, and whether building and the diary feel worth it.
